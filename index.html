From script to animatic.

Previz Lab breaks your script into shots, boards them with camera moves, plans the set in 3D and cuts an animatic you can watch. It all runs on your own computer.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

What it does

One project carries a film from the page to a cut, step by step. You can stop at any step and take the result with you.

1 · ScriptScript to shots

Drop in a script. It is broken down into scenes, characters, locations and a numbered shot list you can edit.

2 · LookOne style, same people

Pick a look for the whole film. Characters and locations are drawn once and kept the same from shot to shot.

3 · PlanThe set in 3D

A floor plan and a 3D room for each location, made from a description, a photo or a frame. Move the camera like in a game engine.

4 · BoardCamera moves

Every shot drawn with its size, angle, lens and movement. Change the camera of any shot from inside the 3D room and draw it again.

5 · AnimaticA cut you can watch

The board timed and cut together at 24 frames a second, with the dialogue under each frame.

6 · ExportTake it to the edit

Shot list as CSV, the board as Word and PDF, the frames, and a timeline for DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro (XML and EDL).

VideoOne change, many shots

Drop a clip or a rough edit, split it into shots, say what changes once (“fire in every compartment of the cabinet”) and approve the look on stills before any video is made.

AI GenPictures, clips and sound on demand

A workbench with nine tabs: images, painted-over edits, upscaling, video, a node canvas, audio, reference sheets, a 3D stage for camera and blocking, and an agent that turns a brief into a storyboard. It runs on Higgsfield and OpenRouter, and every run is priced first and held to your limits.

AssistantA chat that works the project

A panel inside the app that reads, writes and draws with you. Claude Desktop and Claude Code can drive the same app over MCP.

Your computer, your accounts

Projects, frames and clips are saved in a folder on your computer, and nothing is uploaded to us. Making pictures and video uses your own Higgsfield sign-in or your own OpenRouter key; the script breakdown and the assistant use your own Anthropic or OpenRouter key. You pay those services directly, and you set spending limits in the app. A placeholder mode sends nothing to Higgsfield and spends no credits, so you can learn the steps first. The app opens in Slovenian; English is one click in the top bar.

Previz Lab for Windows

Download for WindowsPrevizLab-win-x64.zip · 220 MB

It needs

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • About 570 MB of disk space once unzipped
  • No installer and no admin password: it is a folder you unzip and run

Previz Lab for Windows is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns you the first time. If Smart App Control is on (some new Windows 11 PCs), it can block unsigned apps entirely.

First time on Windows

Unblock the zip

Right-click PrevizLab-win-x64.zip, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom, click OK. Doing this before you unzip avoids most warnings.

Unzip it

Right-click the zip, Extract All, and pick a folder you will keep, such as Documents. Do not run it from inside the zip.

Run anyway

Open the “Previz Lab” folder and double-click Previz Lab.exe. If a blue window says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Keep it handy

Right-click Previz Lab.exe, Send to, Desktop (create shortcut). To update later, replace the folder with the new one: your projects stay.
